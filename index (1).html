Sample-to-dossier lab coordination

Independent: I coordinate the process, and accredited laboratories do the testing.

  1. Sample delivery. Sampling plan, labelling, chain of custody, dispatch.
  2. Lab shortlist. Accredited laboratories whose scope covers the methods you need.
  3. Test parameters. Chosen by ingredient type and destination market.
  4. Quote comparison. Like-for-like on methods, turnaround, sample size and reporting.
  5. Shipping. To the laboratory or directly to the buyer.
  6. Results follow-up. Chase results and resolve questions on methods and units.
  7. Consistency check. Results cross-checked against supplier documents, earlier batches and your specification before anything enters the dossier.
